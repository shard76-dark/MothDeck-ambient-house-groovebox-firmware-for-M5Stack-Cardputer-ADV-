Flash MothDeck

Use earphones. This firmware does not play through the Cardputer ADV internal speaker.

This button writes the full-flash image. It replaces whatever is on the chip, including Launcher. Use Chrome or Edge. Plug the Cardputer in over USB. The application image that Launcher installs is still mothdeck-cardputer-adv.bin. Do not point this button at a TTGO T8.

After it boots, the Settings line says the version. The page list will not offer Exit, because this image replaces Launcher. Hold grave does nothing until a Launcher image is installed again.

The rest of the instrument is in the manual. Nothing on this page has been tried on a Cardputer.
